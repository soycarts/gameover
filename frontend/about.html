GAMEOVER

A fan-built analytics layer for robot combat footage. It watches a clip, works out where the blows land, and draws a retro arcade HUD over the top.

What it does

A vision model reads the frames of a short fight clip and rates what it can see — which machine took a blow, how hard, and roughly where on the frame it landed. Everything after that is ordinary deterministic code: the damage totals, the hit count, the knockout count-out and the health bars are arithmetic, not model output. The result is a JSON timeline, and the page animates the HUD from it in sync with the video.

Alongside that it shows aggregated crowd sentiment — how the r/battlebots thread for a given episode was leaning before the fight, and a couple of representative takes. Comments are shown as quotes and are credited to the subreddit. No usernames are stored or displayed. Where the pipeline needs to tell two commenters apart it uses an opaque salted token, and the salt is not in the repository.
