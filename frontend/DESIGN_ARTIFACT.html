Green Means Fine

Five pixel robot heads stand in for one robot's health. They drain left to right and shift green → lime → yellow → orange → red as the damage stacks up, so the color tells you how bad it is before you ever read the number.

The color is blended continuously between ramp stops, so a bot at 62 HP sits partway between lime and yellow rather than snapping between tiers. The glow, the slider handle, the label, and the panel accent all follow the same value — one number drives the whole mood of the widget.
