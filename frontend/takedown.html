Takedown

Email abuse@gameover.fyi and the content will be removed within 24 hours.

That is the whole process. No form, no counter-notice, no argument, and no need to establish anything first. If you would rather the entire site went dark instead of individual clips, say so and it will.

What helps

Nothing is required, but if it is to hand: the URL of the page, or the name of the fight. That is enough to find it.

What this site is

An unaffiliated fan-built analytics layer over short clips of robot combat footage. Footage © BattleBots Inc.; this project is not endorsed by or affiliated with them. It is non-commercial — no advertising, no donations, no accounts, nothing for sale. There is more detail on the about page.

Personal data

No usernames are stored or displayed, and no accounts exist, so there is no personal data held about visitors or about the people quoted. If you believe something here identifies you, the same address applies and the same 24 hours.
